How to send an automatic reply email from Google Forms

Google Forms can email you when someone responds, but it has no built-in way to email a confirmation to the person who filled it out. Here are the three ways to do it, from simplest to most complete.

Option 1: "Send responders a copy" (built in, very limited)

  1. Open your form and click Settings.
  2. Under Responses, turn on Collect email addresses.
  3. Set Send responders a copy of their response to Always.

This sends a plain copy of the answers. You can't change the wording, add your business name, or say when you'll call back. Many people never notice it.

Option 2: Write a short Apps Script (free, about 30 minutes)

Every Google Form can run Apps Script. Open the form, click the three dots, and choose Script editor. Then add a function that runs on form submit:

function onFormSubmit(e) {
  var email = e.response.getRespondentEmail();
  MailApp.sendEmail(email, "We received your request",
    "Thanks for reaching out! We'll get back to you within one business day.");
}

Next, open Triggers (the clock icon) and add a trigger for onFormSubmit on the On form submit event. Approve the permissions prompt.

This works, but it only covers the reply. You still won't get a formatted alert, and nothing reminds you when a lead sits untouched for a day.

Option 3: A ready-made script with alerts and reminders

If the form is how customers reach your business, a missed or late reply usually means a lost job. Our Google Forms Auto-Reply + Lead Reminder script installs in about 15 minutes and adds:

  • a friendly auto-reply in your business's voice
  • an instant alert to you with the lead's name, phone and message
  • a reminder if a lead is still marked "New" after 4 hours
  • one polite follow-up to the lead after 48 hours if nobody has replied
  • a test mode, so you can try everything on yourself first

It runs entirely inside your own Google account. There's no subscription and no third-party app.

Common problems

  • No email arrives: check that Collect email addresses is on and that the trigger exists under Triggers.
  • "Authorization required": the first run needs you to approve Gmail access. Click Advanced, then Go to project.
  • Emails land in spam: keep the subject plain and send from the same Google account that owns the form.
