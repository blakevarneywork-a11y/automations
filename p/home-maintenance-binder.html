Home Maintenance Binder: Printable Checklists and Logs

A 12-page printable binder for homeowners: monthly and seasonal checklists, home records, contacts and a repair log, in US Letter and A4.

Get it ($7)

Houses mostly fail slowly: a gutter that overflows for two winters, a filter nobody changed, a water heater that was never flushed. This binder puts the routine jobs on paper so they actually get done, and keeps the records you go looking for when something breaks.

  • A Monthly checklist with a box for every month, for alarms, filters, drains and leaks
  • Spring, Summer, Fall and Winter checklists with a "date done" column and blank rows for jobs your own house needs
  • A Yearly and long-term page for the bigger jobs on a longer cycle (water heater flush, chimney sweep, septic, alarm replacement), with last done and next due
  • Home information: where the water and gas shutoffs and the electrical panel are, filter sizes, paint colors
  • Contacts for utilities, insurance and the trades, with account numbers in one place
  • Appliances and systems: model and serial numbers, purchase dates and warranty end dates for repair calls and claims
  • A Repair and service log and a Projects and wish list page. Print as many copies as you need

Clean, ink-light pages that print well on a home printer. Write on them by hand, punch them for a binder, or fill them in on a tablet with any PDF app that lets you write on a page.

What you get: Home-Maintenance-Binder-Letter.pdf (US Letter), Home-Maintenance-Binder-A4.pdf (A4), a short printing guide, and a license for your own home.

Requirements: a PDF reader and a printer, or a tablet with a note-taking or PDF markup app.

Good to know: the timings are general guidance for a typical house. Follow your manufacturers' instructions, and call a licensed pro for gas, electrical and roof work.
